# SamenKlaar product spec and delivery plan

Last reviewed: 18 October 2026.

## Product spec

SamenKlaar is an agent-managed home-services marketplace launching in Amsterdam. Every customer and every professional has an assistant from their first message. The two assistants handle a request between them: they find a professional, negotiate price and visit time, book, confirm, and deal with moves and cancellations. Each assistant talks to its own person in a short chat, asks for what only that person knows (an address, when they are free, a yes or no), and stops to ask whenever something falls outside the limits that person set. Humans decide where a decision needs them: initial PayPal approval, and completion/payment release unless the customer grants per-booking autonomy, anything outside a person's settings, and disputes and money for the operator team. The job keeps the catalog-backed quote, appointment, approved scope and changes, completion evidence, and payment approvals together.

The professional is the service provider contracting with the customer. SamenKlaar operates the marketplace and coordination platform. An assistant acts for a person only through the same validated actions a person could press, inside limits that person can see, change and pause. AI may interpret what people write, summarize, suggest, remind, and request missing information. It cannot invent prices, make a diagnosis, claim to verify workmanship, widen an assistant's limits, or authorize money movement. A customer approves the plan and price (by naming a budget, or by saying yes to the price) and the visit time (by choosing it or saying yes, unless they allow booking without asking); completion and payment are the customer's own decisions.

### Launch decisions

- **Market:** Amsterdam, Netherlands. Start in a focused service area and expand when supply and support can serve it reliably. Accept an address, postcode, neighbourhood or city for matching. Keep exact addresses private until a professional is selected, then reveal them only to that assigned professional. Professionals set a private base and travel radius; broad area results need a full-address coverage check before booking.
- **Services:** plumbing and carpentry are bookable; electrical work is assessment-only. Triage unsupported or unclear work rather than fabricate a final repair quote.
- **Supply and matching:** review professionals before they become bookable. Review identity and business information, PayPal onboarding, and relevant category-specific qualifications or insurance. Show one best-fit professional with an offer derived from their published catalog, then allow customers to view alternatives.
- **Assistants (agent-managed):** on by default for every account, with a default limit set (customer: the budget named in the chat, Monday to Saturday 08:00 to 20:00; professional: auto-confirm and auto-accept inside their working hours with a 90 percent price floor). A person can pause their assistant, set a price ceiling, days and hours and an address, allow booking without asking, and hand over approval of finished work. Payment can never be handed over. Assistants send at most one price counter and one visit proposal each per request, then the people decide. Their messages to a person are private to that person; their negotiation lines are shared and localized for each reader. Assistants have short bot names and a bot icon.
- **Accounts:** customer access is available by default. A person may also have professional access after onboarding and approval. Operators are separately provisioned. Every request and action is scoped to the signed-in account and role.
- **PayPal account types:** customers use their own account during checkout without seller onboarding. Eligible individual professionals should be able to connect personal PayPal accounts, alongside business accounts, through before-payment PayPal Checkout. Netherlands casual-seller support is documented by PayPal; platform seller Intent must be Mixed and both account types must be verified in sandbox before this is considered delivered. Company details apply where relevant to professional review; account connection never bypasses required qualifications, insurance, identity checks or operator approval. See [PayPal account configuration](PAYMENTS.md#personal-accounts-business-accounts-and-sandbox-logins-20-october-2026).
- **Operator administration:** searchable customer/professional account lists, account access and activity, published service coverage and waitlisted demand. Operators can suspend or restore customer/professional access and revoke app sessions with a recorded reason and audit history. Restoring access preserves the prior approval status; operator access remains managed by the service owner. Existing booked jobs require separate review when a professional is suspended.
- **Languages:** customer, professional and operator screens, the assistants' chat and emails support Dutch and English; each assistant speaks its person's language. Dutch copy is a draft until a native speaker reviews it.
- **Customer profiles:** customers edit their display name, optional phone and private usual address/postcode/city from their profile; the sign-in email is read-only. Existing job addresses remain unchanged. Saving a PayPal wallet is blocked until PayPal enables platform vaulting; normal checkout remains available.
- **Booking:** the customer's assistant asks when the customer is free, compares it with the professional's open times, offers what fits, and books after a yes (or on its own if the customer allows it). A customer can also choose a published slot themselves. Professionals publish slots from weekly working hours (with time between visits, minimum notice and how far ahead) plus days off. They can let their assistant accept proposed appointments outside weekly hours; days off, existing bookings, late-change review and payment rules still apply. Individual one-off availability is retired. Hold it while the professional has two business hours to confirm (implemented as weekdays, 09:00–17:00 Europe/Amsterdam). On decline or timeout, release the slot and offer alternatives. A multi-trade request may become separately scoped and quoted jobs on one shared timeline.
- **Work and evidence:** each bookable service defines completion evidence. Missing or unclear documentation blocks completion approval. AI can flag evidence gaps but cannot certify work. A reported incomplete or damaged job pauses capture and opens an operator case; people decide rework or refund.
- **Payments:** use PayPal marketplace payments. Let the customer choose whether PayPal authorizes the agreed total during booking or after the customer reviews completed work (the default; bookings made by an assistant always use the later path until PayPal vaulting is available); before the booking-time authorization succeeds, allow the customer to switch to the later path. A booking-time authorization must succeed before the professional confirms the appointment. Capture only after the customer separately approves completion and payment. PayPal authorizations last up to 29 days, with the highest capture success during the first 3 days; if an authorization cannot be used later, request a new customer-approved authorization. No deposits in the first release. Professionals must complete PayPal onboarding before paid bookings. No platform fee for now (decided 17 October 2026). A later percentage fee must be disclosed before booking and needs unit-economics work and PayPal fee entitlement first.
- **Cancellations:** free customer cancellation or rescheduling through 24 hours before the appointment, which assistants handle on request in the chat (late changes, changes touching a payment hold, and anything after work started stay with people and the operator); later requests go to review, with no automatic cancellation fee in the first release. If a professional cancels, offer rebooking or a refund path. Provide a self-service statutory withdrawal path and record the customer's request and acknowledgment when work starts during the withdrawal period; encode urgent-service exceptions only through reviewed rules, never an AI guess.
- **Notifications:** in-app and email for booking updates, an email and a banner when an assistant needs its person, 24-hour appointment reminders, overdue evidence/approvals, and operator escalation when work is stalled for 24 hours. SMS and WhatsApp are later.
- **Reviews:** only for completed, paid jobs; mark them as verified jobs and allow a professional response.
- **AI configuration:** provider-agnostic, using `AI_PROVIDER`, `AI_PROVIDER_KEY`, and `AI_MODEL`. Secrets stay server-side. The provider also reads free-text replies to an assistant's question as untrusted text and returns only a sanitized intent; a built-in English and Dutch parser is the fallback. A person's address is never sent to the provider.
- **Product experience:** production accounts and routing; no runtime demo identities, fake professionals, placeholder evidence, or simulated payment success. Local test data is an explicit tool (`make seed-pros`: ten named professionals with different trades, areas, prices, free times and assistant settings, on the reserved `.test` domain). Test distinct people in separate authenticated browser sessions.

### Delivery phases

#### Phase 1 — Close launch dependencies

1. Confirm PayPal marketplace product, partner approval process, Netherlands seller onboarding, supported currencies, settlement, platform fees, refunds, and disputes.
2. Set the initial Amsterdam service coverage from actual professional supply; do not assume the entire city is covered.
3. Turn the agreed professional review into an operator checklist, with category-specific qualification and insurance requirements defined by policy owners.
4. Have the professional-as-provider contract model and Dutch/EU withdrawal, cancellation, and refund wording reviewed by qualified counsel.
5. Keep PayPal live payments disabled until required marketplace approvals and integration gates are satisfied.

**Research completed:** the best-fit public PayPal product is the Complete Payments Platform for marketplace checkout and partner fees, with seller onboarding before payment. This is a technical direction, not confirmation of SamenKlaar's approval. The Netherlands eligibility table includes business sellers onboarding before payment for Checkout and Expanded Checkout. PayPal requires platform approval for live Complete Payments Platform calls; sandbox testing is available before approval. Authorization/capture is a supported checkout model, but seller onboarding after payment does not support it. Confirm the exact seller features and settlement behavior with PayPal before treating the direction as final.

#### Phase 2 — Complete trusted supply and booking

Finish professional onboarding and approval, catalog-backed matching, location privacy, Dutch/English interaction, alternatives, quote detail, service checklists, multi-trade requests, temporary appointment holds, two-business-hour confirmation, and release/rebooking on decline or timeout.

#### Phase 3 — Build the resolution workflow

Add post-booking AI follow-up, reminder and escalation timing, overdue evidence/approval handling, scope-change approvals, missing-evidence blocks, cancellation/rescheduling lifecycle, and operator dispute resolution. AI gathers and summarizes facts; humans decide disputed outcomes.

#### Phase 4 — Make money movement and operations launch-ready

Integrate approved PayPal marketplace seller onboarding and payouts, platform fees, verified and idempotent webhooks, delayed/out-of-order reconciliation, authorization expiry, voids/refunds, disputes, notification delivery and production operational controls. Verify full payment recovery before allowing live money.

#### Phase 5 — Prove readiness with real account boundaries

Run account-isolation and end-to-end acceptance across separate customer, professional, and operator sessions. Verify booking timeout, provider decline, cancellation, missing evidence, dispute pause, notification retries, webhook replay, refund, and uncertain payment recovery. Keep external-service contract tests distinct from live provider verification.

#### Phase 6 — Agent-managed requests

Assistants for customers and professionals: a pure decision engine, a bounded runner, versioned limits, an audit log and open questions with an in-app notice; price and visit-time negotiation between the two assistants; the customer's chat (address, budget, availability, offered times, booking after a yes, move and cancel in free text, asking before approving finished work); the professional's private chat (asking before acting outside their settings, reporting what it agreed, the customer's approval and payment status); free-text reading by the AI provider with a built-in fallback; short bot names. Delivered in four slices on 17 and 18 October 2026; see [AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md](AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md).

#### Phase 7 — Agent-made payment holds (blocked)

Let a customer's assistant place a PayPal hold at booking inside a spending authorization (per-job and total caps, expiry), with the person keeping approval of finished work and capture. Blocked until PayPal enables vaulting on the platform account; design and the sandbox probe are in [AGENT_PAYMENT_HOLDS.md](AGENT_PAYMENT_HOLDS.md).

## Implementation status

Updated 19 October 2026. This status describes the current repository (branch `redesign-v2`); it does not certify a live-money release. The consolidated delivery summary is in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).

- **Phase 1:** the operator professional-review checklist, public PayPal dependency register, and sandbox marketplace path are implemented. Professionals onboard and verify seller eligibility; checkout binds authorization and customer-approved instant capture to that seller, with seller-scoped refunds and onboarding/revocation webhooks. Sandbox payments were exercised with a real PayPal sandbox buyer and seller in the 17-18 October sessions: a hold at booking then capture, capture after completion, a full refund, a late-cancellation approval and the void of a live hold on cancellation. PayPal partner approval, fee and settlement setup, initial neighborhood coverage, category-specific vetting policy, and legal review remain open external decisions.
- **Phase 2:** account-scoped Amsterdam matching, address privacy, provider offers, quote and visit negotiation, appointment reservation, two-business-hour professional confirmation (computed in the appointment display timezone; the required Amsterdam clock is still to do), expiry, decline, and rebooking are implemented, plus weekly working hours with generated free times, days off, and several jobs under one parent request. The Dutch/English preference covers customer, professional and operator screens and the assistants; native Dutch review remains. Service-specific evidence policy is implemented with operator approval.
- **Phase 3:** coordination follow-up questions after booking with a reviewable summary, late-cancellation, late-reschedule and withdrawal cases, customer issue reporting, operator case file (claim, contact, wait, close, documentation decisions), evidence and payment gates, scheduled reminders, and escalation for stalled states are implemented. Consumer policy versions are published by operators and acknowledged by customers; the product ships no default legal text. Statutory withdrawal handling and category-specific escalation rules still require reviewed policy.
- **Phase 4:** verified and deduplicated webhooks, capture and dispute reconciliation, full-refund retry, partial-refund/reversal review, authorization expiry and fresh approval, an account-scoped in-app inbox, SMTP delivery with operator-visible delivery health, payment recovery, per-account API throttles and per-client sign-in throttles are implemented. Platform fees, confirmed production settlement, live payment, broader edge-level abuse controls and multi-replica rate coordination are not.
- **Phase 5:** `make check-flow` passes the controller tests, Svelte diagnostics and build, Go tests, vet and build, deployment-script tests and the persisted HTTP/PostgreSQL journeys (both timing choices, negotiated bookings, authorization recovery, replay, cancellation release, and the assistant journeys). A completed, paid, non-refunded job supports one verified review and one professional response. On 18 October the customer and professional assistant chat was exercised live with the real AI provider (Gemini): booking dialogue, negotiation, the professional's confirm question. Not yet exercised live: approving finished work and payment through the assistants, move and cancel in free text with real accounts, operator screens with real data, a real wallet approval of a marketplace payment in production.
- **Phase 6:** delivered (four slices) and covered by unit tests, the availability parser tests, the AI request contract and persisted journeys. Per-booking autonomy can review evidence and release an existing buyer-approved hold. Limits: initial and replacement holds require the buyer, late and paid changes stay with people and the operator, no push notifications, no ranking of matches by rating or routed travel time, no memory of a person's availability across requests, no reminder when a question goes unanswered.
- **Phase 7:** blocked on PayPal enabling vaulting on the platform app (the sandbox calls are refused with `NOT_ENABLED_TO_VAULT_PAYMENT_SOURCE`).

Phase 1's implementation includes the sandbox marketplace path: professional onboarding, eligibility verification, seller-bound authorization, customer-approved capture with instant seller disbursement, seller-bound refunds, and seller onboarding/revocation webhook handling. The platform merchant ID, attribution code, and webhook ID are present in ignored local configuration; the sandbox account flow has been exercised, but production validity and settlement have not been confirmed with PayPal. Public PayPal documentation and the product dependency register are complete, and the operator review checklist is implemented with approval blocked until all checks are complete. The checklist records statuses and brief rationales only; no identity or qualification documents are stored.
