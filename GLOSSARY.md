# SamenKlaar

SamenKlaar coordinates an agreed service between a customer and a professional.

## Language

**Booking autonomy**:
A customer's permission, given for one booking and its agreed price, for their assistant to check completion evidence, approve documented work and release an existing payment authorization. The customer can withdraw it before payment release starts; unclear evidence or changed terms require their decision.
_Avoid_: Unlimited autonomy, automatic trust

**Payment authorization**:
The customer's approval in PayPal to hold an agreed amount for a particular booking. It permits later payment release while valid and does not itself pay the professional.
_Avoid_: Payment, deposit

**Payment release**:
Capturing an authorized amount to pay the agreed professional after completion approval. It does not authorize a new charge or a larger amount.
_Avoid_: New payment authorization
