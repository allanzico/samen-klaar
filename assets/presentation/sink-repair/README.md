# Fictional repair fixtures

`before-broken-trap.png` and `after-replaced-trap.png` are a matched AI-generated image pair used in the recorded product walkthrough. The first shows an open break in a removable sink U-bend; the second shows a replacement between the same existing pipes. They are illustrations, not photographs of a real repair or evidence of a real water test.

Use the before image for customer intake. For the fictional professional completion record, upload **both images** and the explicitly fictional `work-note.txt`. Private customer intake photos are separate from completion evidence. The application still applies its normal service-scope, evidence, consent and payment guards; there is no presentation bypass.

`prompts.json` preserves the generation prompts. These assets are distributed under the repository's MIT license to the extent applicable. Do not use them to claim real work or obtain real payment. The walkthrough uses PayPal sandbox wallets and displays generated-image disclosure.
